// Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 26, 2026 · Last revised: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled in mobile applications developed and published by piodev on Google Play.

> Applications Covered

This Privacy Policy applies to the following applications by piodev:

  • Memory Trash Truck, Clean City (com.moewballs.memory.trash.truck.clean.the.city) — see section 6h
  • Kula Zgadula (com.moewballs.polski.quiz.znawcow.magii) — previously published as "Polski Quiz Znawców Magii"; same application, the package name has not changed
  • Color Match Car (com.moewballs.colormatchcar)
  • Tank 2000: Play Together (com.moewballs.tank2000playtogether)
  • Quiz u Janusza (com.moewballs.janusz)
  • Panda Drink: Boba Tea (com.moewballs.pandadrink)
  • Mini Horde (com.moewballs.minihorde) — offline game; progress is stored only on your device; shows optional rewarded ads via Google AdMob (see sections 3 and 3c)
  • Excavator Memory Game for Kids (com.moewballs.memory.excavator) — designed for children, see section 7a

Applications may be displayed under different localized titles depending on the user's language settings on Google Play. All localized versions refer to the same applications identified by their package names listed above.

> 1. Data We Collect

We operate no servers of our own and our applications do not transmit data to us. The data listed below is processed only on your device, unless a third-party service is involved (such as advertising — see section 3, and only in applications that display ads):

  • Technical data (processed on your device only): Device model, operating system and version, application version, random identifier (e.g. UUID) used to distinguish local profiles
  • App usage data (stored locally only): Game results, gameplay statistics, game progress — saved on your device and never transmitted to us
  • Profile data (stored locally only): Some applications let you create a local player profile (e.g. a nickname, an avatar, and optionally a date of birth used for entertainment features such as horoscope-style content). This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server
  • Personal data: Not collected automatically. Personal data may only be provided voluntarily by the user (e.g. via email contact)

Applications do not collect personal data automatically, unless you consent to it.

> 2. Purpose of Data Processing

Collected data is used solely for:

  • Ensuring proper operation of the application
  • Saving game progress
  • Improving gameplay and user experience
  • Diagnosing and fixing technical issues
  • Displaying ads and rewards (if the application uses ads)

The data is not used to identify individual users.

> 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Some of our applications may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process the following data:

  • Device advertising identifier (Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location based on IP address
  • App usage and ad interaction data
  • Device information (model, OS version)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

This data may be used by Google to personalize advertisements shown to the user. Not all applications use advertising. If an application does not currently display ads, this section does not apply to that application.

> 3a. Ad Personalization and Opt-Out

Advertisements displayed in our applications may be personalized based on your interests, app usage, and device information. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising → disable "Personalized Ads"

Please note that opting out of personalized ads does not reduce the number of ads shown; it only means the ads will be less relevant to your interests.

> 3b. Tracking Technologies

Our applications do not use cookies. However, third-party services such as Google AdMob may use advertising identifiers and similar tracking technologies on your device to deliver and measure advertisements. These identifiers can be managed or reset through your device settings as described above.

> 3c. Rewarded Ads

Some of our applications offer optional rewarded ads: you may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for a small in-game bonus (for example, a hint or a small amount of in-game currency). Watching rewarded ads is always voluntary and requires your explicit action — ads of this type are never shown without your consent. Rewards are virtual items usable only inside the application, have no monetary value, and are non-transferable.

> 3d. Ad Consent (EEA / UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, before any ads are shown the application will display a consent dialog (Google's consent management platform) asking whether you agree to personalized advertising. You can decline and still use the application, and you can change your choice later in the application settings.

> 4. Data Sharing

User data is not sold and is not shared with third parties, with the exception of:

  • Advertising partners: If the application displays ads, advertising partners may collect technical device data for ad personalization
  • Legal authorities: If required by law (e.g. court orders)

> 5. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, and alteration:

  • Encryption during data transmission (if applicable)
  • Access to data is limited to authorized persons only

> 6. Local Storage

Our applications may store game progress and settings locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to any server and remains only on your device.

> 6f. Device Motion Sensors (Panda Drink)

Panda Drink: Boba Tea uses the device's orientation sensor (accelerometer / gyroscope) solely to detect how the phone is tilted, so the virtual drink can be "poured" and "drunk" by tilting. Sensor readings are processed in real time on the device only; they are never recorded, stored or transmitted. The app also uses the vibration motor for short haptic feedback (can be disabled in settings). Saved drinks, settings and screenshots you choose to save remain on your device. The app works fully offline.

> 6g. Excavator Memory Game for Kids

Excavator Memory Game for Kids is a memory card game for young children. The game works fully offline; an internet connection is used only to load advertisements. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data ourselves: there are no user accounts, no chat, no sign-in and no links to social networks. Game progress, stars, coins and settings (sound, vibration, language, graphics quality) are saved only locally on the device and are removed when the application is uninstalled. The app uses the vibration motor for short haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings). The application displays advertisements from Google AdMob (a banner below the game board and a full-screen ad between finished boards) and does not offer in-app purchases. Advertising in this application follows the rules for children's apps described in section 7a, which take precedence over section 3 for this application.

> 6h. Memory Trash Truck, Clean City

Memory Trash Truck, Clean City is a memory and match-3 puzzle game for players aged 13 and over; it is not designed for children (the optional "With a child" section contains simple games intended to be played together with an adult and shows no advertisements). The game works fully offline; an internet connection is used only to load advertisements. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data ourselves: there are no user accounts, no chat, no sign-in and no links to social networks. Game progress (districts, coins, fleet, night results) and settings (sound, vibration, language, graphics quality) are saved only locally on the device and are removed when the application is uninstalled. The app uses the vibration motor for short haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings).

The application displays advertisements from Google AdMob: a banner below the game board, a full-screen ad between finished boards and nights, and optional rewarded ads (for example to double a reward or to call reinforcements at night) that are shown only after you tap a clearly marked button, as described in section 3c. Ads are limited to content rated for teens. Section 3 applies to this application, including the use of the device advertising identifier. In the EEA and the UK a consent dialog is shown before any ads (section 3d) and your choice can be changed at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. Settings also offer "Write to us" (opens your e-mail application with the game version and device model pre-filled in the subject line; nothing is sent unless you send the message yourself), "Rate the game" (opens the game's page on Google Play) and, after selected results, a "Share" button that uses the system share sheet with an image generated on your device — none of these send any data to us. Google Play may occasionally show its own in-app review dialog; it is provided and handled by Google. The application does not currently offer in-app purchases; if an optional "Remove ads" purchase is added in a future version, section 6d will apply and this policy will be updated.

> 6a. Camera and QR Codes

Some of our applications can use the device camera solely to scan QR codes (for example, to join a local multiplayer room created by another player). The camera image is processed on your device in real time only for code detection. No photos or videos are taken, stored, or transmitted anywhere. The camera permission is optional — if you deny it, the application remains fully usable and the same action can be completed without scanning (e.g. by selecting a room from a list).

> 6b. Local Network Multiplayer

Some of our applications offer a local multiplayer mode in which devices connect directly to each other over the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot (local room discovery and a direct connection between devices). In this mode, your chosen nickname and in-game results are visible to the other players in the same room. This communication stays entirely within your local network — it does not pass through the internet and is not sent to us or to any external server.

> 6c. Notifications

Some of our applications can show optional local notifications (for example, reminders about in-game events). These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device — no push server is involved and no data is collected or transmitted for this purpose. Notifications are off by default or require your explicit permission (Android 13+), and can be disabled at any time in the application settings or in the system settings.

> 6d. In-App Purchases

Some of our applications offer optional in-app purchases of virtual items (for example, in-game currency or hints). All payments are processed by Google Play's billing system. We do not receive, process, or store your payment details (such as card numbers). Virtual items are for use within the application only, have no real-world monetary value, and are non-transferable. Refunds are handled in accordance with Google Play's refund policy.

> 6e. Google Play Games Services (Leaderboards, Achievements, Cloud Save)

Some of our applications offer an optional integration with Google Play Games Services. Signing in with your Google Play Games profile is voluntary — the application remains fully usable without it (your progress is then stored only locally, as described in section 6).

If you choose to sign in, the following applies:

  • Sign-in and player identity: Google processes your Play Games player ID, gamer nickname, and Play Games avatar in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store this data on any server of our own (we operate none).
  • Leaderboards (public): When you submit a score, your result is published on the game's leaderboards together with your Play Games gamer nickname and a small gameplay tag consisting of: your in-game avatar, your in-game house, and — only if you chose to set it in your profile — the region (voivodeship) you declared. This information is visible to other players of the game. The region is a manual declaration made by you; the application does not read or use your device location. Whether other players can see you on public leaderboards at all is controlled by your Play Games profile visibility settings (Google Play Games app → profile → settings).
  • Achievements: Unlocked achievements are stored in your Play Games profile and are subject to the same profile visibility settings.
  • Cloud save: Your game progress (profiles, unlocked content, in-game currency) can be backed up as a saved-game snapshot in the application data area of your own Google account (Google Drive application data). This snapshot is accessible only to this application through your account; it is not accessible to us and is never transmitted to any server operated by us.
  • Deleting this data: You can delete your Play Games profile data for this game (including cloud saves) at any time in the Google Play Games app: Settings → "Delete Play Games account & data" → delete data for an individual game. Uninstalling the application removes all locally stored data.

> 7. Children's Privacy

Unless stated otherwise below, our applications are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

> 7a. Applications Designed for Children (Excavator Memory Game for Kids)

Excavator Memory Game for Kids is designed for children and participates in Google Play's Designed for Families program. For this application:

  • We do not collect any personal information from children (no name, e-mail, location, photos, contacts or device identifiers are collected by us).
  • All game data (progress, stars, coins, settings) stays on the device only.
  • There are no social features, chats or user-generated content. The only external link (this privacy policy) is placed behind a parental gate.
  • Advertising. The application shows ads from Google AdMob, an ad network certified for Google Play's Families program. Only two formats are used: a banner below the game board and a full-screen ad between finished boards — never when the app starts and never during play. There are no rewarded ads.
  • Every ad request is tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (GDPR), and is limited to ad content rated G (suitable for all audiences). As a result, ads are non-personalized: no interest-based advertising and no remarketing.
  • The application does not access the Advertising ID (the Android permission for it is removed) and does not use the Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs. Because users are treated as under the age of consent, no consent form for personalized advertising is shown.
  • To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may process technical data such as the IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, and interactions with ads. This data is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Any future options for parents (for example removing ads) will be placed behind a parental gate.

Parents or guardians with questions about this application can contact us at the e-mail address in section 10.
